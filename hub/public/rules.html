Voting rules

One eligible account, one equal vote

Allocate exactly 100 integer points across active projects. Your ballot persists until you change it. Only the latest version saved before Monday 00:00 UTC applies to that week's snapshot. Your account must have been created before that Monday and must not be suspended. Accounts created during the week can count from the following Monday.

One-human-one-vote is the goal, not a guarantee. Passkeys, a registration human check and network limits reduce abuse; they do not prove that every account is a different person. Please create only one voter account.

Equal weight

Compute, contributions, tokens and sponsorship never change voting weight. Sponsorship never buys votes. Voted targets, routed assignments, accepted receipts and impact are separate measures. Routed units are work handed out, not measured compute.

Weekly publication

The hourly job at minute 00 publishes the Monday snapshot on its first successful run that week. Shares use integer basis points and largest remainders, totaling 10000 when there are valid ballots. A delayed run uses the Monday cutoff, not later edits. Fewer than 5 eligible ballots, or (rarely) a noisy result with nothing usable, means a clearly labelled provisional fallback carrying the previous allocation. Random noise is added to the published split; this is not a formal differential-privacy guarantee.

If a project becomes inactive, a ballot containing it is excluded until saved again across active projects. A paused project remains eligible for votes; routing can pause when there is no work or reviewer capacity. The scheduler explains gaps and invents no assignments.

Appeals

For a suspension or review appeal, open an issue labelled appeal at github.com/DaryllGomas/swarm-ai/issues (never post personal details there) with your account's short identifier or receipt and the reason for your appeal.
