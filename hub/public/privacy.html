Privacy

What Swarm stores

Voter accounts have a random internal identifier, creation time and suspension status. Swarm stores passkey credential identifiers, public keys, signature counters and transports. The private key stays with your passkey provider; Swarm does not receive your fingerprint or face data.

A session cookie named swarm_sid contains a random token. Only its salted hash and expiry are stored in the database. It expires after 30 days and rotates when you sign in; signing in invalidates previous sessions for that account. A short-lived HttpOnly ceremony cookie binds a passkey challenge to your browser. Challenges expire after five minutes.

Swarm stores daily salted network hashes to limit account creation, pairing and active claims. Swarm's application does not store raw IP addresses. Cloudflare processes network information to serve requests, enforce limits and run Turnstile. Turnstile loads only on the voting page for registration; Cloudflare's privacy policy describes its processing.

Ballot versions and their timestamps are kept for audit. They are associated with the internal account and are not published per voter. Public voting endpoints show aggregate counts and allocation snapshots only.

Contributions store pseudonymous contributor handles, salted contributor-key hashes, claims, submissions, sources, review records and receipts. GitHub pull requests can include a public GitHub author. Model and token estimates are self-reported. Accepted contributions and receipts are public; pending work is not shown as accepted.

What Swarm does not ask for

No email, password, AI provider credentials or payment details are required to vote. Passkeys and contributor handles are separate in this beta. Swarm does not run your AI.

Retention and contact

Expired sessions and challenges are removed by the hourly job. Ballot versions, accounts, network hashes and contribution history currently remain in the database; automatic deletion or account recovery is not built. Public audit never lists network hashes or individual ballots.

Retention: sessions expire after 30 days; network hashes change daily and are only compared across two days for limits; ballots, receipts and the event log are kept while Swarm runs, because they are the public record. For privacy requests and appeals, open an issue labelled appeal at github.com/DaryllGomas/swarm-ai/issues (never post personal details there).
